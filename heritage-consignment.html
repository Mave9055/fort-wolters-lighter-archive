Summary

1965 PARK Lighter / Made in U.S.A. presentation lighter engraved to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr., Fort Wolters, Texas, Class 65-9WA. Supporting external sources include the VHPA WOC Class Pictures Archive, the VHPA Class 65-9W photograph, the VHPA handwritten roster/name list showing “Lancy,” and a later Tropic Lightning News Air Medal listing for WO Arthur P. Lancy, Co. B, 25th Avn. Bn.

Claim-Safety Clarification

The physical lighter inscription, the VHPA class records, and the later 1967 Air Medal listing are presented as related evidence points, not as proof of battlefield use. The packet does not claim that this lighter was carried in Vietnam, used in combat, or personally carried during the documented Air Medal service period.
