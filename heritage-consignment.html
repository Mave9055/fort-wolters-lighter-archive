Executive Summary

This 1965 Park Industries presentation lighter is engraved for WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr. of Class 65-9WA at Fort Wolters, Texas. The front shows the Class 65-9WA designation and Hughes World Leader logo; the reverse is a named presentation inscription.


The auction appeal is provenance-driven: named recipient, specific Army Aviation training class, Fort Wolters location, Vietnam-era context, Hughes TH-55A training association, and supporting documentation images.
