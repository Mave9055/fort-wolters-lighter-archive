Neutral identification

1965 PARK Lighter / Made in U.S.A. presentation lighter connected by inscription to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr., Fort Wolters, Texas, Class 65-9WA. External records support a related Fort Wolters Class 65-9W class-photo set and a roster/name-list marked CLASS 65-9 R/W that includes “Lancy.” A later 1967 newspaper listing identifies WO Arthur P. Lancy, Co. B, 25th Avn. Bn. under Air Medal recipients. These three evidence categories should remain separate.

Auction-house checklist

  • External citations are clickable and independent of GitHub-hosted images.
  • Physical artifact evidence is separated from VHPA class evidence and later service evidence.
  • Page avoids Zippo, Park Industries, exact-value, current-function, Vietnam-carry, and combat-use claims.
  • Condition is stated honestly and does not imply operation.
  • Image paths used on the page: lighter-1.jpg through lighter-6.jpg.
  • Lightbox/magnifier is enabled for image review on desktop; mobile remains readable without magnifier.

Missing items that would strengthen the packet

  • Dedicated close-up of base stamp reading “PARK Lighter / Made in U.S.A.”
  • Dedicated close-up of the complete reverse inscription.
  • Dedicated close-up of the Hughes World Leader logo/marking.
  • Side, hinge, insert, and interior photos.
  • One photo with ruler/scale and one photo under neutral lighting.
  • Any provenance chain: where acquired, prior owner statement, receipt, estate source, or written consignor declaration.
