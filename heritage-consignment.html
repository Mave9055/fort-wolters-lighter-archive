1965 Fort Wolters Class 65-9WA Presentation Lighter Named to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr.

This packet is built for appraiser review. The strongest object evidence is the reverse inscription, which states that Lancy was a member of the first graduating class using the Hughes TH-55A helicopter at the U.S. Army Primary Helicopter Training School, Fort Wolters, Texas.

Neutral identification

1965 PARK Lighter / Made in U.S.A. slim presentation lighter inscribed to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr. The reverse inscription states that Lancy was a “Member, First Graduating Class Using Hughes TH-55A Helicopter” at the U.S. Army Primary Helicopter Training School, Fort Wolters, Texas.

The explicit inclusion of the TH-55A transition milestone directly on a period-engraved presentation lighter represents an exceedingly rare textual variant for Fort Wolters pieces of this cycle.

Outside sources document a related Class 65-9W entry in the VHPA Fort Wolters class-photo archive, a roster/name-list marked CLASS 65-9 R/W including the surname “Lancy,” and a 1967 Tropic Lightning News Air Medal listing for WO Arthur P. Lancy, Co. B, 25th Avn. Bn.

PRESENTED TO
WOC Arthur P. Lancy, Jr.
MEMBER, FIRST GRADUATING CLASS
USING
HUGHES TH-55A HELICOPTER
U.S. ARMY

FORT WOLTERS, TEXAS

What this packet does not claim

  • No Zippo claim. This is identified by the base stamp as PARK Lighter / Made in U.S.A.
  • No current working/sparking claim.
  • No seller-set value or auction estimate.
  • No claim that the lighter was carried in Vietnam or used in combat.
  • No claim that the “A” in 65-9WA means Alpha company. In Army Aviation training pipelines of this era, the "W" designated the Warrant Officer stream and the "A" typically represented the aviation track or school subclass iteration.
  • No claim that the Hughes marking proves official issue by the Army or Hughes Aircraft.
  • The “first graduating class” language is presented as inscription evidence unless independently verified by the appraiser or additional Army aviation records.
