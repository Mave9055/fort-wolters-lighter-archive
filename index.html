1965 Fort Wolters Presentation LighterNamed to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr.

A period-engraved slim presentation lighter from Class 65-9WA — among the first Army Aviation classes to train on the Hughes TH-55A Osage at Fort Wolters, Texas.
