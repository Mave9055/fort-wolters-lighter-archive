Archive Overview

This research archive documents the historical context surrounding the Class 65-9WA presentation lighter named to WOC Arthur P. Lancy Jr. The archive focuses on three interconnected elements:

  • Fort Wolters Class 65-9W (1965): Among the first full classes to train on the Hughes TH-55A Osage helicopter
  • Company B, 25th Aviation Battalion: Vietnam service from mid-1966 through mid-1967
  • Arthur P. Lancy Jr.: Training recipient and later Air Medal recipient

Fort Wolters Class 65-9W (1965)

Historical Significance

Class 65-9W represents a pivotal moment in Army Aviation training history. The Hughes TH-55A Osage was officially adopted as the Army's primary trainer in 1964, and Class 65-9W was among the first full classes to utilize this aircraft at Fort Wolters.

Class Designation: 65-9W / 65-9 R/W
Year: 1965
Location: Fort Wolters, Texas
Aircraft: Hughes TH-55A Osage
Training Type: Primary helicopter training (Warrant Officer Candidate)

Class 65-9WA Presentation Lighter (FW-ARC-0001)

The Class 65-9WA presentation lighter serves as the primary artifact connecting Fort Wolters training to Vietnam service.

Reverse Inscription

PRESENTED TO
WOC Arthur P. Lancy, Jr.
MEMBER, FIRST GRADUATING CLASS
USING
HUGHES TH-55A HELICOPTER
U.S. ARMY
PRIMARY HELICOPTER TRAINING SCHOOL
FORT WOLTERS, TEXAS
1965

Artifact Significance

The explicit inclusion of the TH-55A transition milestone directly on a period-engraved presentation lighter represents an exceedingly rare textual variant for Fort Wolters pieces of this era. The lighter documents Lancy's participation in the first graduating class to train on the Hughes TH-55A Osage, a pivotal moment in Army Aviation training history.
